⚠️ Top Concepts to Know Cold

Marketing definition (AMA)"Activity, set of institutions, and processes for creating, communicating, delivering, and exchanging offerings that have value…" — know this word for word.
The 4 marketing eras in orderProduction → Sales → Marketing Concept → Societal Marketing. Sales Era = promotions & personal selling. Marketing Concept Era = consumer needs focus.
Maslow's hierarchy — exact orderPhysiological → Safety → Love/Belonging → Esteem → Self-Actualization. Bottom to top. Many students flip Esteem and Self-Actualization.
Flat tire = Problem RecognitionThe flat tire scenario always tests the first stage of the CDM. You recognize a need when something unexpected creates a problem requiring a solution.
4 I's — all four, correctlyIntangibility, Inseparability, Inconsistency, Inventory. "Innovation" and "Impression" are wrong — both appear as distractors on the real exam.
SWOT: external = O & TOpportunities and Threats are external (no control). Strengths and Weaknesses are internal (some control). The exam tests this distinction directly.
