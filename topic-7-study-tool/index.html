📌 High-Priority Topic 7 Concepts
Digital channels: distinguish digital marketing from traditional marketing by channel and measurability.
SEO vs. PPC: SEO builds organic visibility; PPC buys immediate search visibility.
Email: direct digital communication with permission, measurement, and legal responsibilities.
A/B testing: change one variable at a time so you can identify what caused the result.
Analytics types: descriptive, predictive, prescriptive, and cognitive answer different business questions.
7-step process: always begin by defining the business problem before collecting or modeling data.
Data-driven advantage: evidence improves predictability, targeting, and resource allocation.
Real-time data: faster signals allow marketers to adapt strategy while conditions are changing.
